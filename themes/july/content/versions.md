## 6.x.x - July (current version)
Release date: July 2025<text class="versions-date"></text>.
- Simply named **July** after the month of its creation.
- The core philosophy here is minimalism-stripping away the clutter to build something as clean and lightweight as possible.
- It still carries the beautiful typography inspired by the **risotto theme**, which has become a staple of my design.

## 5.x.x - Sylvie
Release date: April 2025<text class="versions-date"></text>.
- Designed to mimic the aesthetic of a classic desktop application window, complete with a functional (well, sort of!) close button.
- Continued to use the gorgeous **risotto theme** typography to maintain high readability.

## 4.x.x - Chloe
Release date: August 2023<text class="versions-date"></text>.
- I was introduced to the [risotto theme](https://themes.gohugo.io/themes/risotto/) by a friend and instantly fell in love with its elegant, reading-focused layout.
- I took the original theme, heavily customized it to fit my personal style, and named the result **Chloe**.
